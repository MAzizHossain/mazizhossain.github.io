Gaming

Video games were more than a pastime growing up. This is a small record of the games I played, the ones that stayed with me, and the things I enjoyed about them.

All-time favorites

Anno 1800

I always enjoyed playing city-building games like SimCity and Cities XL, but Anno 1800 takes it further. You build cities across different regions while managing trade, diplomacy, propaganda, and war. I have played most of the other games of the Anno series (except Pax Romana), but Anno 1800 takes the cake for me because it combines the satisfaction of building cities with the complexity of running an expanding empire.

Star Wars: Empire at War

I used to wonder how societies might develop once a species becomes interstellar and expands beyond its home planet. Since people are prone to violence and bound to fight one another, this game made it possible to imagine or at least entertain what warfare might look like on a galactic scale.

Deus Ex: Mankind Divided

I have always enjoyed playing sci-fi games that make me think about where technology might take us. This game stood out to me for its vision of a future shaped by augmentation, inequality, surveillance, and the choices people make when technology changes what it means to be human.

Detroit: Become Human

This game is the opposite of Deus Ex. It ponders what happens when machines become, or perhaps simply act, more human. It made me wonder where we would draw the line between a machine and a person. We have made silicon think, yet we ourselves are made up of atoms. Perhaps consciousness was never meant to have a simple scientific answer.

Honorary mentions (Do not tell the English teachers)

Cyberpunk 2077

Cyberpunk stood out to me because it explores what happens when two consciousnesses fight for control of the same body. I found it strikingly similar to the way microbes and the immune system are constantly trying to outmaneuver each other.

Games I played professionally (or at least convinced myself I was good at).

League of Legends

I discovered this game by accident. I ended up playing on several teams in different roles as I became skilled. My highest achieved rank was Master tier, although I would probably place myself around Diamond 2. I also won a few tournaments in Bangladesh, and in 2018, I got to travel to Sri Lanka to represent my country at the IGE South Asia Cup. We did not win, but it was a fun experience nonetheless.

Counter-Strike: Source

My friends introduced me to this game while we played at gaming zones. Since we were always competing with each other, my skills improved. I participated in a few tournaments, but it was mostly just for fun, goofball experience. Whether this counts as playing professionally is still under review.
